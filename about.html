About the Website

Carolina Golf Course Explorer was created as part of a web development project for MIS 310. The idea behind the website is to create a resource where golfers can discover and compare golf courses throughout North Carolina and South Carolina.

Rather than searching through multiple websites, visitors can use this site as a starting point to learn about different courses and find additional information.

Our Purpose

The purpose of Carolina Golf Course Explorer is to help golfers find courses that match their interests, experience levels, and preferences. Not every golfer is looking for the same type of course.

Some golfers prefer affordable public courses, while others are interested in championship layouts, luxury resorts, or challenging private clubs. This website aims to introduce visitors to these different golfing experiences.

Who Is This Website For?

Carolina Golf Course Explorer is designed for golfers of all experience levels. Whether you are just starting out, looking for a new local course, or planning a golf trip, this website can help you explore your options.

The website can also be useful for visitors traveling to the Carolinas who want to learn more about the golf courses available in different areas.

What You'll Find

The website is organized into separate pages for North Carolina and South Carolina. These pages are designed to provide information about featured golf courses.

  • Golf course names and locations
  • Pictures and descriptions of courses
  • Public and private course information
  • Course features and amenities
  • Golf Digest course rankings
  • Links to official golf course websites

How Courses Are Selected

Golf courses featured on this website are selected using a combination of notable golf destinations, course information, and rankings published by Golf Digest.

Golf Digest publishes rankings of highly regarded courses in each state. However, many of these courses are private, meaning they may not be available to the general public. Because of this, the website also aims to highlight public courses that more golfers can access.

Golf Digest rankings are used as a reference for course recognition and are not rankings created by this website.
