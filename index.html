Welcome to Carolina Golf Course Explorer!

Looking for your next place to play golf? Carolina Golf Course Explorer is designed to help golfers discover courses throughout North Carolina and South Carolina. Whether you are looking for an affordable public course, a challenging layout, or a scenic golf resort, this website is a great place to start.

Explore Golf in the Carolinas

North Carolina and South Carolina offer a wide variety of golfing experiences. From mountain courses to coastal golf destinations, there are plenty of places for golfers of all skill levels to enjoy.

North Carolina Golf

North Carolina is home to some of the most well-known golf destinations in the country. Areas such as Pinehurst, Wilmington, and the western mountains offer golfers a variety of courses with different challenges and scenery.

Explore North Carolina Courses

South Carolina Golf

South Carolina is another popular golf destination, with courses located throughout Myrtle Beach, Charleston, Hilton Head, and Kiawah Island. Golfers can enjoy coastal views, championship layouts, and a wide range of public and private courses.

Explore South Carolina Courses

Featured Golf Course: High Hampton

Located in Cashiers, North Carolina, High Hampton is a scenic mountain golf destination. Surrounded by beautiful mountain views and natural landscapes, the course offers a different golfing experience from the coastal courses found throughout the Carolinas.

High Hampton is one example of the many unique golf destinations visitors can discover throughout North Carolina and South Carolina.
