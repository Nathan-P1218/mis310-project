Welcome to Carolina Golf Course Explorer!

Carolina Golf Course Explorer helps golfers discover courses throughout North Carolina and South Carolina. From mountain golf destinations to famous coastal courses, this website is designed to introduce visitors to some of the best golf experiences in the Carolinas.

Explore golf courses, learn about top-ranked locations, and find links to rankings and course information throughout both states.

Explore Golf in the Carolinas

North Carolina Golf

North Carolina is home to some of the most respected golf courses in the country. From Pinehurst to the western mountains, golfers can experience beautiful scenery and a wide variety of course styles.

Explore North Carolina Courses

South Carolina Golf

South Carolina is known for famous golf destinations such as Kiawah Island, Hilton Head, Charleston, and Myrtle Beach. Golfers can enjoy coastal views, championship layouts, and world-class golf resorts.

Top Course in North Carolina

According to Golf Digest's state rankings, Wade Hampton Golf Club is ranked as the top golf course in North Carolina. Located in Cashiers, this course is known for its beautiful mountain setting and highly regarded design.

Wade Hampton Golf Club is one of the premier golf destinations in North Carolina and represents the mountain golf experience the state is known for.

Top Course in South Carolina

In South Carolina, Kiawah Island Golf Resort is home to one of the state's most famous golf courses. The Ocean Course is especially well known for its championship history, scenic coastal setting, and challenging layout.

Kiawah Island is one of the most recognized golf destinations in South Carolina and highlights the coastal golf experience that makes the state so popular.
